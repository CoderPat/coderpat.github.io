Hey! I’m Patrick.

I'm currently a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University and Instituto Superior Técnico, supervised by Graham Neubig and André Martins. I've also spent some time doing research at Google Deepmind, Microsoft Research and Unbabel.

In a past life, I've have also worked on program analysis with graph neural networks.

I did my masters at the University of Cambridge and my undergrad at Instituto Superior Técnico.
